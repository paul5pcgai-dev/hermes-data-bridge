Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

This policy describes Account Data Bridge, an independent, locally run Hermes MCP connector that accesses a TikTok account only after its holder authorizes the requested scopes. The service is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok.

TikTok information accessed

With TikTok’s approval and the account holder’s authorization, the connector requests only read-only scopes:

  • user.info.basic: account identifier, profile image, and display name.
  • user.info.profile: username, bio, profile links, and verification status.
  • user.info.stats: follower, following, likes, and public-video counts.
  • video.list: public videos posted by the authorized account and metadata TikTok makes available.

These scopes do not provide private videos, direct messages, or individual follower/following lists. They do not grant permission to post or upload videos. Actual access is limited to the scopes TikTok approves and the user grants.

How information is used

Information returned by TikTok is used to answer the user’s request in Hermes. The connector does not use it for advertising, sell it, or use it for unrelated profiling. TikTok receives API requests needed to return the authorized data.

Processing and sharing

The connector runs on the user’s device and has no separate service-side database for TikTok content. Results are returned to the user’s active Hermes conversation. The model provider configured for that Hermes session may process the conversation content under its own terms and retention settings.

Storage and retention

The connector does not persist TikTok profile or video responses after handling the request. The TikTok OAuth credential is stored in the user’s local Hermes credential storage. Hermes may retain returned content in its conversation/session history according to the user’s settings; the configured model provider may process or retain it under that provider’s policies.

To stop future access, revoke Account Data Bridge in TikTok’s account/app settings and remove its local credential from Hermes. Previously returned content may remain in Hermes history or provider records under their respective retention settings.

Security

Access is limited to approved, user-authorized read-only scopes. Users should protect the device and Hermes profile where the OAuth credential and conversation history are stored.

Contact and changes

For privacy or support questions, use the developer contact shown on the TikTok authorization screen. This policy will be updated if the connector’s scopes or data-handling practices change.
